Golden State Builder

A California City Builder

Welcome to Dry Creek Valley — 1,300 acres of unincorporated oak-studded foothills in El Dorado County, California. A river runs through the valley, the hills turn gold every summer, and nobody lives here yet.

Zone land, build roads and services, and attract residents. But this is California: Prop 13 caps your property tax revenue, CEQA reviews slow big projects, RHNA housing mandates arrive once you incorporate, and every year brings fire season, earthquake risk, and the chance of drought.

Reach 500 residents and you can petition LAFCO to incorporate as a real city — unlocking your 1% Bradley-Burns sales-tax share, a city hall, and state housing law politics.
