Golden State Builder

A California city builder · every build

Each version is playable and self-contained — nothing carries over between them, so you can open any build and start a city. Saves are version-specific.

Volume 5 — Public TransitLatest
Draw bus routes along your streets and light rail on its own right-of-way. Commuter rail and high-speed rail stations connect you to the region.

Volume 4 — Housing, Power & Water
Four zoning designations from single-family to mixed-use. Six ways to make electricity, five ways to find water — and what you can build depends on where you settled.
SB 100 clean-electricity mandate · SGMA groundwater limits · hydro, geothermal and desalination gated by geography · day/night toggle
Volume 3 — Subdivision
Roads lead somewhere. Run one out to raw land, drag a parcel, and take it through the process — the recorded tract lays in its own streets and lots.
Subdivision Map Act: Tentative Map → CEQA → Planning Commission → Final Map · neighbor appeals · Quimby, school and traffic impact fees
Volume 2 — Four Californias
Sierra foothills, Central Valley farmland, the Pacific coast, and the high Mojave — each with its own terrain, hazards and climate.
Save and load · elections and recalls · land value and traffic overlays · bridges, avenues, bonds and Measure T
Volume 1 — Dry Creek Valley
Where it started: 1,300 acres of unincorporated oak foothills, a river, and a fire season.
Prop 13 property tax caps · CEQA review · RHNA housing mandates and the Builder's Remedy · wildfires, earthquakes, drought
